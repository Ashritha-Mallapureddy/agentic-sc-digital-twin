# Agentic CX desk

**Open the app:** [ashritha-mallapureddy.github.io/agentic-sc-digital-twin](https://ashritha-mallapureddy.github.io/agentic-sc-digital-twin/)

A customer-experience PMO puts one page in front of leadership: the promise to the customer, and the cost that can break it. In 2026 that cost includes CBAM certificates on steel, fertilisers, aluminium, cement and hydrogen.

Four agents read the same sample book.

- The customs reader holds the import lines.
- The chain scout names the installations behind the invoice, including the coal, ore and gas the importer does not buy.
- The emissions clerk uses a verified figure where it exists and the marked country default everywhere else.
- The certificate desk ranks those gaps by euros and by the customer promise they hold open: the delivery date (Liefertreue), the order change (Auftragsänderung), and the surcharge complaint (Reklamation).

The certificate is still paid. What the desk removes is the mark-up that exists only because an installation is missing.

Linea Automotive GmbH is a sample declarant. The installation names and their actual figures are synthetic. Country defaults, the mark-up schedule, the CBAM factor and the Q3 2026 certificate price of €82.32 follow the published method. A filing uses the Commission Excel. This is not an employer system.
